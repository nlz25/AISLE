# World relief map

Land geometry: Natural Earth, 1:110m land, public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

`world.svg` and `world-lights.svg` are generated by `python3 scripts/build_map.py` with Pillow (see `requirements-dev.txt`). The map shows land only, without political boundaries. Markers represent regional research groups, positioned near their participating institutions; they are not precise facility locations.

Relief uses the existing Solar System Scope / INOVE surface, packed elevation and historical night textures, licensed CC BY 4.0 and credited in `public/assets/globe/credits.txt`. The red elevation channel supplies directional hill shading, combined with desaturated surface detail. Both images are cropped to 85°N–60°S at 2400 × 1120, matching the map's equirectangular projection and interactive pins. Each WebP is embedded in a self-contained SVG and clipped to the same Natural Earth land polygons; the coast outline remains vector.

`world-lights.svg` holds a transparent city-light layer derived from the historical night texture. Background suppression, a brighter intensity curve and two soft warm bloom radii reveal the original city distribution. This layer uses screen blending above the terrain, so the terrain's dark colour filter cannot dim the lights. It does not indicate research coverage. No repeating dot pattern, invented terrain or political borders are added. Ordinary builds use the checked-in SVGs and need no imaging dependencies.
