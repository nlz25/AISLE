What does the score mean?

Fixed thresholds and task-specific transformations define the scores. A score of 44.73 is an aggregate index, not a 44.73% success rate. Scoring principles ↗

What is being compared?

First-stage models run through the study’s experimental interaction protocol. Future evaluations of complete agents will identify the model, harness, tools and resource budget explicitly.

Select a model to inspect its category scores, weighted contributions and coverage. Raw trajectories and exact invocation settings are being prepared separately. Bayesian optimization baselines belong to applicable task-level comparisons and do not enter these aggregate rankings.

Coverage and missing results

GLM 5.1 and DeepSeek V4 Pro were not included in the study’s multimodal evaluation. They remain in the shared-task index and are excluded from the full-task index. This is a statement about this study, not the capabilities of an entire model family.

Repeated runs and uncertainty

Most method–condition combinations have three runs. Small score differences do not establish statistically significant superiority. The study reports raw errors, counts and repeat-level outcomes alongside its scoring rules.
