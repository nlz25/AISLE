AISLE Earth imagery

Optical flare textures: Three.js r183 lens flare example, credited to ro.me.
https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_lensflares.html
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r183/examples/textures/lensflare/lensflare0.png
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r183/examples/textures/lensflare/lensflare3.png
Stored as lensflare-glare.png and lensflare-ghost.png without file changes.
Only lensflare-ghost.png is used at runtime. The broad glare bitmap is archived.
The source bloom is a numerical Gaussian texture; the static source is a CSS
radial gradient. Aperture RGB is converted to additive alpha in memory, and
optical drawing preserves the canvas's existing alpha instead of covering
the background. Static fallbacks use the same aperture texture as a mask.
Three.js MIT license: ../vendor/globe.LICENSES.txt

Changes: converted to WebP for delivery. No geographic features were repainted.
Initial 2048 x 1024 surface and 1024 x 512 packed terrain/cloud maps are
downsampled from those same originals, keeping the first and HD frames coherent.

Land/ocean material mask: derived from the packed source's green channel above.
The land mask is thresholded, resized to 1024 x 512, quantized to sixteen coast
coverage levels and stored losslessly as earth-land-mask.webp. It supplies stable
sea/land material classification at every surface-texture resolution.

Night imagery pyramid: Solar System Scope / INOVE's original 8192 x 4096
night map, under the same CC BY 4.0 license above.
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/8k_earth_nightmap.jpg
Changes: resized to a 1024 x 512 global base and 2K/4K/8K tiled WebP levels,
with neighbouring-image gutters for filtering. City lights were not repositioned.
The complete source remains outside website delivery.

Standard surface, clouds and historical city lights: NASA Goddard Space Flight
Center; Reto Stockli and Robert Simmon. NASA Earth Observatory, The Blue Marble
(2002). These are illustrative historical textures, not live satellite data.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/
