# Spectral-inference demonstration

The visualization uses the synthetic data in assets/synthetic-spectra.json, with molecular rendering, measurement gates and a conditional pKa fit. It is an illustrative task demonstration rather than a new measured dataset.
The Agents / Models tiles show equally presented examples, evaluated separately. They do not represent a collaborating multi-agent system or claim that every displayed model participated in this illustrated run. Brand icon sources and licence information are recorded in src/icons/SOURCES.json and src/icons/LICENSE-lobe-icons.txt. Three.js is bundled with its upstream licence header.
